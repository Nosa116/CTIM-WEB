What to Expect When You Visit

Warm Welcome

From the moment you walk through the doors, you'll be greeted by friendly faces ready to assist you and find you a comfortable seat.

Worship & Word

Engage in vibrant, uplifting praise and worship followed by practical, life-applicable teachings rooted firmly in the scriptures.

CTIM Kids

We provide safe, age-appropriate, and engaging environments for your children to learn about God's love while you enjoy the main service.

Come As You Are

There is no dress code. Whether you dress up formally or wear casual jeans, you'll fit right in with our diverse community.

Thank you for planning your visit. A dedicated host team member will reach out to you via email and phone to coordinate meeting you on the day of your visit!
