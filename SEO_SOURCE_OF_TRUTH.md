5. **The Second Coming:** Rapture of the church and the second coming of Christ.

7. **Personal Salvation:** Regeneration by the power of the Holy Ghost through the Word of God.
8. **Divine Healing:** Redemptive work of Christ providing healing for the body and salvation for the soul.
9. **Empowerment for Service:** Baptism and receiving of the Holy Ghost giving enablement for Christian service and witness.
10. **Sanctification:** Sanctifying power of the Holy Ghost unto God and from the world.
11. **Final Resurrection:** Final resurrection of both the saved (eternal life) and the lost (eternal judgment).
