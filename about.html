Who we are

Connecting You to
Faith, Community, & Hope

Christ Temple International Ministry was founded with a clear, heaven-born mandate: to make Christ known, to establish believers in their faith, and to build a vibrant, loving community that reflects the light of God. Over the years, we have grown into a diverse family representing people from all walks of life, united by our devotion to Jesus.

"We are not just a Church; we are a family where you can belong, grow, and serve the purpose of God for your generation."

Whether you are seeking answers, looking for a spiritual home, or desiring to deepen your walk with God, Christ Temple is a place where you will receive sound biblical teaching, experience genuine fellowship, and find opportunities to make a difference in our world.

Our mission

Christ Temple International Ministry exists to preach the uncompromised gospel of Jesus Christ, build a community of committed disciples, and prepare them for His soon return — reaching the world with the transforming message of love, healing, and salvation.

Our vision

Christ Temple International Ministry sees lives transformed by the power of the Holy Spirit, establishing strong families, vibrant local assemblies, and a lasting legacy of faith that influences generations to come.

What We Believe

01

The word of God

We believe that the bible is the inspired and infallible word of God.

02

The trinity

We believe that there is one God eternally existent in the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost.

03

Deity of Christ

We believe in the deity of Christ; that he was born of a virgin; conceived of the Holy Ghost and that he lived a sinless life.

04

Resurrection & ascension

We believe that he died; that he rose again from the dead bodily and ascended into heaven bodily.

05

The second coming

We believe in the rapture of the church and the second coming of Christ.

06

Cleansing from sin

We believe that the only means of being cleansed from sin is repentance and faith in the precious blood of Jesus.

07

Personal salvation

We believe that regeneration by the power of the Holy Ghost through the word of God is essential for personal salvation.

08

Divine healing

We believe that the redemptive work of Christ on the cross provides divine healing for the body and salvation for the soul to everyone that believes.

09

Empowerment for service

We believe in the receiving of the Holy Ghost giving divine enablement for Christian service and witness.

10

Sanctification

We believe in the sanctifying power of the Holy Ghost unto God and from the world.

11

Final resurrection

We believe in the final resurrection of both the saved and the lost. The former to eternal life and the latter to eternal judgement.
